# Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026.

Spotter is built so the sensitive parts of a workout stay on your iPhone or iPad. This policy explains what the app processes, what stays on the device, and what is sent to a service we use.

[Support](support.md) · [Terms](terms.md)

## Who we are

Spotter is published for iPhone and iPad. Privacy questions go to [muzaffarumar768@gmail.com](mailto:muzaffarumar768@gmail.com).

## Information that stays on your device

- **Camera and pose.** During a workout, Spotter uses the camera and Apple’s on-device Vision framework to estimate body position, count reps, and score form. Workout video is not recorded and is not uploaded.
- **Workout history.** Sessions, sets, form scores, and the weights you log are stored on the device with Apple’s on-device database.
- **Progress selfies.** Photos you take for a before-and-after stay in the app’s storage on the device. They are shared only if you create a video and send it yourself through the iOS share sheet.
- **Music.** Workout music plays songs already saved on the device. Spotter does not upload those songs and does not require an Apple Music subscription.
- **Plan settings.** Goal, training place, level, units, and reminder time are stored on the device.

## Location

If you allow it, Spotter uses your location only while you are using the Gyms tab, to find nearby gyms and draw a route. Location is not used to build an advertising profile, and it is not added to workout analytics.

## Analytics

Spotter uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which screens and controls are used, for example starting a workout or opening a tab. Events can include the screen name, exercise name, goal, training place, level, weight unit, and whether Spotter Pro is active.

Analytics does not include workout video, progress selfies, body weight, height, or precise location. Firebase is operated by Google. Google’s privacy information is at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Spotter uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service to deliver workout reminders and progress-photo reminders. That uses a device push token. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Purchases

Spotter Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store with StoreKit. Apple processes the payment. Spotter receives whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your card number. Apple’s privacy policy is at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name or email inside the app.
- We do not use workout video or progress selfies for advertising.
- The app is not directed at children under 13.

## How long information is kept

On-device workouts, settings, and selfies remain until you delete them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Analytics events are kept by Firebase under Google’s retention settings for the project. Push tokens are replaced when the system issues a new one and stop being useful when you turn notifications off or delete the app.

## Your choices

- Deny camera, location, photo, or notification access in iOS Settings. The rest of the app still opens.
- Turn off reminders in Spotter Settings.
- Delete the app to remove on-device workouts and selfies.
- Email [muzaffarumar768@gmail.com](mailto:muzaffarumar768@gmail.com) with a privacy request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of Spotter after the new date means the updated policy applies.
