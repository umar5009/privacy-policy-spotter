## Camera and form coaching

Prop the phone up about 2 to 3 metres away, in bright even light, and step into the frame. Pose detection runs on your device. Workout video is not recorded or uploaded.

If the camera stays off, open iOS Settings → Spotter → Camera and allow access. In the iOS Simulator, Spotter shows an animated demo instead of a live camera.

## Subscriptions

Spotter Pro is billed by Apple. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial when you are eligible. Prices shown in the app come from the App Store.

Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Workouts, selfies, and reminders

- Workout history stays on this device. Deleting Spotter deletes that history.
- Progress selfies stay on this device until you choose to share a video.
- Daily workout reminders and progress-photo reminders can be turned off in Spotter Settings, or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
- Goal, training place, and level can be changed anytime in Settings → Your plan.

## Nearby gyms

The Gyms tab uses your location only while that screen is open, to show nearby gyms and a route. You can deny location access and keep using the rest of Spotter.
