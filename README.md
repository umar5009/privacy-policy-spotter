## Support

Spotter is a personal trainer for iPhone and iPad. It counts reps, scores your form, and keeps your workouts on your device.

Email [muzaffarumar768@gmail.com](mailto:muzaffarumar768@gmail.com) and include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when something went wrong.

### Camera and form coaching

Prop the phone up about 2 to 3 metres away, in bright even light, and step into the frame. Pose detection runs on your device. Workout video is not recorded or uploaded.

If the camera stays off, open iOS Settings → Spotter → Camera and allow access. In the iOS Simulator, Spotter shows an animated demo instead of a live camera.

### Subscriptions

Spotter Pro is billed by Apple. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial when you are eligible. Prices shown in the app come from the App Store.

Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

### Workouts, selfies, and reminders

- Workout history stays on this device. Deleting Spotter deletes that history.
- Progress selfies stay on this device until you choose to share a video.
- Daily workout reminders and progress-photo reminders can be turned off in Spotter Settings, or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
- Goal, training place, and level can be changed anytime in Settings → Your plan.

### Nearby gyms

The Gyms tab uses your location only while that screen is open, to show nearby gyms and a route. You can deny location access and keep using the rest of Spotter.

## Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026.

Spotter is built so the sensitive parts of a workout stay on your iPhone or iPad.

Spotter is published for iPhone and iPad. Privacy questions go to muzaffarumar768@gmail.com.

### Information that stays on your device

- **Camera and pose.** During a workout, Spotter uses the camera and Apple’s on-device Vision framework to estimate body position, count reps, and score form. Workout video is not recorded and is not uploaded.
- **Workout history.** Sessions, sets, form scores, and the weights you log are stored on the device.
- **Progress selfies.** Photos you take for a before-and-after stay on the device. They are shared only if you create a video and send it yourself.
- **Music.** Workout music plays songs already saved on the device. Spotter does not upload those songs.
- **Plan settings.** Goal, training place, level, units, and reminder time are stored on the device.

### Location

If you allow it, Spotter uses your location only while the Gyms tab is open, to find nearby gyms and draw a route.

### Analytics and notifications

Spotter uses Firebase Analytics for screens and controls you use, such as starting a workout. Events can include the screen name, exercise name, goal, training place, level, weight unit, and whether Spotter Pro is active. Analytics does not include workout video, progress selfies, body weight, height, or precise location.

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple push deliver workout and progress-photo reminders.

### Purchases

Spotter Pro is sold by Apple. Spotter does not receive your card number.

### Your choices

Deny camera, location, photo, or notification access in iOS Settings. Delete the app to remove on-device workouts and selfies. Email muzaffarumar768@gmail.com with a privacy request.
